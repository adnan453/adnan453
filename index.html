Who We Are

About Nezeth International

Nezeth International Private Limited is an India-based agricultural import & export company.

We specialize in supplying export-grade fruits, vegetables, and spices sourced from trusted farms across key agricultural regions in India, delivering them to international markets with reliable logistics and professional export documentation.

With a commitment to quality and transparency, we connect Indian producers with global buyers, ensuring every shipment meets international standards and buyer expectations.
